Miniscore

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy covers Miniscore for Android, iPhone and iPad, with package or bundle identifier com.ivicastojanoski.miniscore, operated by Ivica Stojanoski. For privacy questions, contact s.ivica@gmail.com.

Using Miniscore

Miniscore shows basketball scores and player statistics. You do not need an account. The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or device location. The current app does not include advertising, in-app purchases or an app analytics SDK. Game searches and matchup selections are processed on your device and are not sent to our game server.

Network requests and service logs

The app connects over HTTPS to our Railway-hosted service to download game data. Our service and its hosting provider receive technical information needed to handle the connection, including your IP address and request information. Operational access logs may include the request time, requested path and response status. We use this information to deliver the service, diagnose failures and protect it against abuse. We do not use it to build advertising profiles or sell it.

Depending on the app version, team logos and player photos may be downloaded directly from ESPN or NBA image servers. These providers receive your IP address, the requested image address and technical request headers when an image is downloaded. Their handling of these requests is subject to their own privacy policies. Images already bundled with earlier releases do not require a network request. Current releases, including the iPhone and iPad app, show plain team codes and no player avatars. They do not download team or player images, even if image URLs appear in a game feed.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the last successfully downloaded game feed and may cache downloaded images for offline use. Searches and the selected matchup may also remain in saved app state. Clearing Miniscore's app storage removes its local data; uninstalling removes app data from the device. Android or Apple backup and device-transfer services may retain or restore eligible app data depending on your device settings. You can manage those copies through your device's backup settings.

Contacting support

For help using Miniscore, visit our support page or email s.ivica@gmail.com.

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use that information to answer your question, investigate the issue and keep a record of its resolution. Please avoid sending passwords or other sensitive information.

Railway provides hosting for game data, GitHub Pages hosts this policy, and our email provider handles support correspondence. Providers may process information in countries other than your own. We may disclose information when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the security of the service. Opening an external link subjects your visit to that site's privacy practices.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local game data remains until replaced by a refresh or removed through your device settings; image caches can also be evicted automatically. Our game database stores game content, not individual user accounts or search histories. Railway's current Hobby plan provides seven days of operational log history; the provider's own security records and retention practices are governed by its policies. Support correspondence is retained for as long as needed to handle your request, document its resolution or meet applicable legal obligations.

You can request access, correction or deletion of personal information we hold by emailing s.ivica@gmail.com. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law, including any required retention. There is no Miniscore user account to delete. To avoid fresh network requests, you can use previously cached game data while your device is offline.

Security

Game data and remote images are requested using HTTPS. We restrict access to the private owner dashboard. No method of transmission or storage can guarantee complete security.

Children

Miniscore is intended for teenagers aged 13 and above and adults, and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can address it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and revise the date above. Material changes will also be communicated within the app when required.
